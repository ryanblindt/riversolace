Ellijay, Georgia

A Georgia mountain cabin on the river, with a hot tub

Plenty of North Georgia cabins have a hot tub. Fewer sit right on the water. River Solace has both.

What makes River Solace different

  • On the Ellijay River: waterfront at the end of a private road, with a big covered porch facing the water.
  • Private hot tub on the deck: soak with the river running just below.
  • Fire pit by the river: plus a gas grill and picnic table with extra lighting.
  • Porch dining and fishing: eat outside and cast a line right off the porch.
  • Two king beds, two baths: sized for two couples or a quiet trip for two. Adults only, up to 4 guests, 25 and older.
  • Dog friendly: fenced yard, no pet fee, no breed or weight limits.
  • Cozy inside: gas log fireplace, full kitchen, fast Wi-Fi, and a TV with the NFL package.

It is a good fit for anniversaries, couples' weekends, fall leaf trips and winter escapes. It is not a party house: no events, and quiet is the point.

Where to stay

River Solace, right on the Ellijay River

River Solace is a private 2-bedroom, 2-bath log cabin on the Ellijay River for up to 4 adults. Soak in the private hot tub on the deck, fish right off the porch, cook on the gas grill and eat on the covered porch, then end the night at the fire pit by the river. Dogs are welcome with no pet fee, and the TV includes the NFL package for game days.
